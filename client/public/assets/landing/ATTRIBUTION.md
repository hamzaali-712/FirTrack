# Open Anatomy attribution

The two static SVG illustrations are simplified adaptations of the male and
female models published by [open-anatomy-3d](https://github.com/MervinR07/open-anatomy-3d).
They are original vector interpretations, not direct mesh-to-SVG exports. The
underlying models and catalog are declared CC BY 4.0 by that project.

> Anatomy: BodyParts3D © The Database Center for Life Science (CC BY 4.0);
> female organs: Human Reference Atlas 3D Reference Organs, HuBMAP (CC BY 4.0);
> female body: MakeHuman (CC0). Modified by open-anatomy-3d
> (https://github.com/MervinR07/open-anatomy-3d), CC BY 4.0.

The FitTrack SVGs have been simplified and modified. They are static illustrations,
not the original 3D assets, exercise animations, anatomical guidance, or
diagnostic material. No GLB model or animation is bundled in the landing page.
