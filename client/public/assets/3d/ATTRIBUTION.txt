The pack states that it is MIT licensed; the included LICENSE.txt preserves
that notice. Its README says the figure was generated with MPFB2 and the
motions come from pose definitions. The source asset library identifies the
human as CC0-1.0 and the Pushup motion as a CC0-1.0 Mesh2Motion clip, retargeted
to the MakeHuman default rig. This upstream rights chain has not been
independently audited.

The animation is an illustrative preview only. It has not been independently
reviewed for exercise form and is not coaching, medical, or safety guidance.
